Case Study

DIAG MEDICAL

Always-on digital marketing for healthcare services.

SCOPE
Managed always-on digital campaigns for healthcare services across multiple channels.
ROLE
Media planning, campaign execution, daily optimization, reporting, and cross-functional collaboration.
IMPACT
Achieved lead and CPA targets while maintaining stable campaign performance through continuous optimization.

Planning

Monthly bottom up plan, allocate budget for 3 platforms for 10 products

Execution

Facebook Structure Campaign: ABO for controlled short-term push, CBO for stable long-term growth

Testing Phase

Optimize for learning accuracy

Creative Testing

1 : 1 : N
Budget: CBO Adsets: 1 Ads: Multiple

A single adset removes audience as a variable, so CBO can allocate spend freely across creatives — the algorithm concentrates data on the strongest performer faster, giving a cleaner read on creative alone.

Audience Testing

1 : 4 : N
Budget: ABO Adsets: 4 Ads: Multiple

Fixed budget per adset under ABO ensures every audience gets an equal, uncontaminated spend — a fair head-to-head comparison that CBO would otherwise skew toward whichever audience wins early.

Scaling Phase

Optimize for speed & stability

Aggressive Scale

1 : N : 1
Budget: ABO Adsets: Multiple Ads: 1

A single validated creative is duplicated across many adsets; ABO caps spend per adset, so growth is pushed hard while each cell's performance stays isolated and easy to diagnose.

Neutral Scale

1 : N : 5
Budget: CBO Adsets: Multiple Ads: 3-5

With several adsets and a portfolio of validated creatives, CBO auto-shifts budget to what's working in real time — a smoother, lower-risk scale once early volatility has already been resolved.
